How an enquiry works
प्रारम्भिक संपर्क की प्रक्रिया

A simple path from enquiry to consultation.

STEP 01

Send basics

प्रारम्भिक जानकारी दें

Share your name, contact and a short description of the matter.

STEP 02

Identify the issue

विवाद की पहचान

Identify the property, civil, revenue or commercial issue involved.

Discuss documents

Bring relevant orders, deeds, notices, records or pleadings as applicable.

Decide next action

A suitable next step can then be discussed based on the facts and documents.
